# HomeBulk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 September 2026_

HomeBulk does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Everything you enter (equipment, workouts, sets, bodyweight) is stored only on your device.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.
- Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.
- Tapping "Watch a demo" opens YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app; YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.
- Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset all data, removes everything.
